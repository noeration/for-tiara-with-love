## Editable layers

- All body copy, headlines, labels and footers are Canva text boxes.
- Page backgrounds, rules and decorative shapes are separate elements.

## Known deviations from wireframe

Canva's generator built the layouts. The structure and copy follow the approved blueprint, but these details differ:

- Page 5 background renders as a deeper cobalt than `#2B3BEE`; text contrast stays high.
- Decorative icons and the step path were generated by Canva, not drawn from the wireframe.
- Slide 1 notification sits lower and smaller than the wireframe version.
- Slide 2 question bubbles are wider than the wireframe.
- Slide 6 uses the rebuilt single-page layout, so its composer and notification are placed closer to the page edge than the wireframe.

If you want pixel-level alignment with the wireframes, the next pass is manual layout in the Canva editor, not another AI generation.
